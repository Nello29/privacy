Privacy Policy – Amazon Ads

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This policy describes how Antonello Russo handles data accessed through a personal Amazon Ads tool (the Login with Amazon application used to connect to the Amazon Ads API).

Who we are

This tool is operated by Antonello Russo, an independent publisher of non-fiction books. Contact: tilterpublishingltd@hotmail.com.

Who uses the tool

The tool is used only by Antonello Russo, on the Amazon Ads account owned by him. It is not offered to the public and does not access any other person's Amazon account.

What data it accesses

With the account owner's authorization, the tool accesses the advertising data of that account: campaigns, ad groups, keywords, bids, budgets, and performance reports (impressions, clicks, spend and sales). It uses them only to review the performance of his own book ads and to manage those campaigns. The tool does not collect personal data about shoppers or readers.

Where data is kept

The tool runs on the owner's own computer. The access credentials issued by Amazon are stored on that computer only. Reports downloaded to work on them are kept on that computer.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share this data with third parties, and we do not use it for any purpose other than managing his own advertising. When the owner asks an AI assistant (Anthropic's Claude) to work on specific reports or campaign settings, that data is processed by the assistant on the owner's instruction, under its provider's terms.

Revoking access

The account owner can revoke the tool's access at any time from the Amazon account's Login with Amazon permissions, or by writing to the contact address above. Revoking it makes the stored credentials useless.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
